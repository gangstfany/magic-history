# AP Art History Unit 5 source ledger

This ledger audits the 27 ordered media identities required by `data/ap-art-history-unit-5-manifest.json`. The current College Board CED fixes work and view identity only; it is never treated as a public image host. Every reusable image is traced to a direct HTTPS asset and an explicit permission statement. A visible web image is not considered reusable by itself.

For living Indigenous cultures and culturally sensitive works, photographic copyright and cultural authority were reviewed separately. Restricted rows keep the authoritative source page but omit the image URL from the public bundle. The AP 153 relief remains restricted because the CED credits a rights-managed Corbis photograph and the openly licensed cactus-bearer relief could not be proven to be the same panel. Yaxchilán Structure 40 remains restricted because Peabody terms do not provide a portable public-release license. Great Serpent Mound and the Kwakwa̱ka̱ʼwakw mask are also restricted on both rights and community-care grounds.

Identity authority: [College Board AP Art History Course and Exam Description](https://apcentral.collegeboard.org/media/pdf/ap-art-history-course-and-exam-description.pdf). Content and terminology checks are documented in [art-history-sources.md](../art-history-sources.md).
