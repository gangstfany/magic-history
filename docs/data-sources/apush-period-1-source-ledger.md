## Scope and exclusions

- 1619 material is excluded because it belongs to Period 2, not this release.
- Known Hudson/Northwest Passage inaccuracies are excluded: this release does not claim that Henry Hudson discovered the Northwest Passage or that Hudson Bay connects to the Mississippi.
- 1491 is used as the APUSH course boundary for sustained Columbian contact, not as a claim that no European had ever arrived earlier.
- Port Royal (1605) and Quebec (1608) are distinguished; neither is substituted for St. Augustine (1565).
- The data does not generalize English-colony religious freedom or self-government into Period 1 or across all colonies.
